Fresh parts

Why buy fresh

  • Full warranty and a proper invoice
  • Known quality and a guaranteed fit when genuine
  • Longer life, fewer repeat repairs
  • Best for safety parts: brakes, tyres, belts, steering
Used parts

Why buy used

  • Usually much cheaper than new
  • Handy for older cars with discontinued parts
  • Good for body panels, mirrors, lights, trim and interior
  • Lets you save money on non-critical parts

Get the most benefit

  1. Note your car's make, model, year and fuel type, and the old part number if you can.
  2. Compare the same part on two or three sites before you buy.
  3. Ask for a GST invoice and check the return and warranty policy.
  4. Prefer genuine or well-known brands for safety parts. Avoid used brake pads, tyres, belts and airbags.
  5. For used parts, inspect in person, check for cracks or wear, and ask the seller to test it.
  6. Have a trusted mechanic confirm the fit before fitting anything you are unsure about.

Redline Garage is not affiliated with these sites. Links open the dealer's own website; prices, stock and seller quality vary, so check reviews before you pay.

General guidance only. Always follow your owner's manual, and ask a qualified mechanic if you are unsure.
